# Execution-order action decoder

Design proposal, 2026-09-18. **Implementation is in progress; compiled parity and learning evidence are still required.** The [RL review](REVIEW_RL_20260918.md) reports 98.05% scripted-v27 development score for the BC initialization: the current actor can represent strong play. Prefix conditioning addresses a representational restriction; it is not an established explanation or cure for subsequent PPO deterioration. The completed [cross-run comparison](RUN_COMPARISON_20260918.md) selects the baseline; the [architecture campaign design](ARCHITECTURE_ABLATIONS_20260918.md) places this decoder alongside shared-plan and feed-forward forecasting alternatives.

## Current boundary and proposed model

[`EntityActor`](src/kaggriculture/entity.py#L574) emits all unit and market preferences from one observation. [`act_batch`](src/kaggriculture/policy.py#L347) and Rust [`Game::select_factors`](rust/kagg_env/src/core.rs#L1856) subsequently select actions in order and update legality. Later masks reflect earlier choices, but relative preferences among remaining legal actions do not. Quantity scores condition on selected kind, without the complete selected prefix.

Introduce an explicitly identified causal actor family through [`Architecture`](src/kaggriculture/registry.py#L29). Reuse the public spatial/economic encoder in [`EntityTrunk`](src/kaggriculture/entity.py#L345), separating encoded source memory from its current decision-slot reasoning. Encode once per turn and cache source K/V projections. Use a small causal decoder, initially one or two layers, with 36 ordered factors: 16 unit actions, then ten market orders, each choosing kind followed by quantity.

Each query combines slot/type identity, a shifted selected-action embedding, and features of the ledger immediately before that decision. Cross-attention reads cached public memory. Quantity queries see the just-selected kind; later market queries see previous quantities and their effects. Inactive units and quantities, and slots after STOP, have canonical padding and zero likelihood weight. Their tokens must not introduce arbitrary padding-dependent behavior.

The actor receives both farms' public state and its own private inventory, as today. Opponent-private inventory or critic representations must never enter its encoder, ledger, or decoder cache. The critic remains independently parameterized. A turn-local prefix cache is not temporal memory across turns.

## Exact legality on the device

The current native boundary accepts all scores at once. A causal actor cannot reuse it unchanged: each selected action affects the next neural query. Calling Rust after every factor would introduce up to 36 host/device synchronization points per turn.

Export an exact policy-state bundle from Rust and implement batched device-side ledger operations. Keep integer legality state separate from normalized BF16 neural features. Required fields include positions and active units, seeds, shed contents, unit inventory and insertion order, relevant tile state, day, money, hires and original unit count, land ownership, market inventory, and configuration constants.

Match [`UnitLedger`](rust/kagg_env/src/core.rs#L444) and [`PolicyMarketLedger`](rust/kagg_env/src/core.rs#L767), rather than reimplementing the entire environment. Important details include:

- Planting reserves shared seeds; earlier units can alter a later unit's tile legality. Watering, harvesting, digging, structures, animal placement and fertilizer state therefore require exact prefix updates.
- Capacity-limited DROP follows inventory insertion order. Quantities alone cannot reconstruct the resulting shed. Pickup legality deliberately excludes partially fillable requests; preserve the trained policy action space.
- Market orders change balances, capacity, hires, land and cumulative prices. [`market_price`](rust/kagg_env/src/core.rs#L3208) uses nonlinear float64 calculations and ties-to-even integer rounding. At the price floor, selling does not advance inventory in the usual way.

Prefer an exact integer price lookup generated by the native implementation over a float32 approximation. Establish a finite reachable inventory range from supported configuration and action limits, account for its memory cost, and reject unsupported ranges rather than clamping. Prove lookup equality at rounding boundaries and floor transitions.

Do not reconstruct exact state from structured replay features. [`_money_feature`](src/kaggriculture/tokens.py#L413) logarithmically transforms cash, tile features clip information, and rollout storage reduces precision. Introduce a versioned exact-state schema separately from observation features.

## Training, sampling and storage

Teacher forcing supplies the complete selected sequence with shifted inputs and causal attention, so all neural decoder positions can train together. Predecision ledger features are generated by deterministic prefix replay; they contain no consequences of the current or future action. This ledger preprocessing remains sequential even though the neural forward is parallel.

Generation uses fixed-size K/V buffers and a compiled fixed-length loop on the GPU. Keep encoder memory, ledger, masks, selected factors and likelihoods on device until the turn finishes. Transfer final factors together. Extend native supplied-factor stepping with reusable output buffers and builtin-opponent handling; existing [`factor_masks`](rust/kagg_env/src/python.rs#L128) and [`step_factors`](rust/kagg_env/src/python.rs#L821) provide oracle and execution starting points.

[`RolloutBatch`](src/kaggriculture/rollout.py#L84) already stores actions, masks, active flags and behavior conditional log probabilities. Add either exact initial policy state or predecision ledger features; measure storage/transfer cost before choosing. Do not store learned hidden states for PPO replay: current parameters must recompute them from the recorded prefix.

Update both [`_replayed_component_logprobs`](src/kaggriculture/ppo.py#L1356) and [`_replayed_selected_logprobs`](src/kaggriculture/ppo.py#L1392), including parity diagnostics. Sum active conditional log-ratios for joint PPO. Existing component clipping in [`_policy_sums`](src/kaggriculture/ppo.py#L2313) is a distinct surrogate; keep that experimental choice explicit.

Update [`_clone_loss`](scripts/train_bc.py#L837) to teacher-force the same prefixes. BC archives retain [`raw_json_zlib`](scripts/extract_bc_dataset.py#L144), allowing exact ledger regeneration without recollecting demonstrations. Version derived caches. Integrate orientation transforms, frozen opponents, evaluation and export; add new modules to [`PACKAGE_FILES`](scripts/build_submission.py#L46). Keep the sampler semantics identical across these paths.

## Gates before a learning comparison

1. Compare every GPU mask and prefix ledger against Rust across full seeded games and targeted shared-tile, insertion-order, capacity, rounding, price-floor, STOP and inactive-unit cases. Separately check intended versus executed actions; native market settlement includes opponent interactions beyond the policy ledger.
2. Prove sequential and teacher-forced conditional likelihood parity under production BF16 compilation. Perturb later actions to establish causality, earlier actions to establish conditioning, and opponent-private inputs to establish actor privacy.
3. Exercise BC, PPO updates, frozen opponents, orientations and exported inference through the same ordering. Use [`policy_compile_options`](src/kaggriculture/model.py#L170); CPU or eager neural execution is not acceptable training/rollout validation.
4. Benchmark full production batches and horizons through MLQ, measuring encoder reuse, compilation, peak memory, replay storage and synchronization. Reject per-factor host round trips. Only then queue full-budget learning with the fixed external panel, both decoding modes, actor-update counts and score per GPU-hour.

Promote the decoder only on measured gameplay and compute evidence. Passing parity establishes implementation fidelity, not improved learning.
