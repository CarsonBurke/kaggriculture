The older outcome-summary file contains stale queue states and should not override them. There is one training seed; game-seed intervals do not quantify training-seed uncertainty. The tile-bias checkpoint has undergone far fewer waves, so its advantage could reflect slower forgetting as well as spatial inductive bias.

The hardness run recorded approximately 58,127 mean training money over its final twenty waves, versus 50,599 for control. Opponent mixes and ending iterations differ, so this is descriptive telemetry. Source-read improved held-out critic fitting. Neither observation establishes stronger deployed play. The requested default promotions retain those useful changes but the combined-default learning comparison remains missing. The completed historical and candidate evaluations cover both sampled and argmax execution against the same panel. Training samples at temperature 1; [deployment uses argmax](../../src/kaggriculture/inference.py#L422). A strong sampled result and weak argmax result would implicate this mismatch; weak results under both would instead point toward forgetting, objective coverage, or faulty credit. That distinction must precede an architecture ranking. Opponent-specific scores matter more than an overall score averaging a saturated starter panel with v27.

The follow-up also checked sampled training telemetry (`artifacts/probes/credit-valuation-20260918/historical-training-telemetry.json`). With the BC actor frozen during critic warmup, control/source scored 28.83% over 333 v27 games; hardness scored 26.92% over 208. In the final twenty waves they scored 0%, 0%, and 1%, respectively, over 100 v27 games per arm. These changing-map, unpaired training samples cannot replace the fixed evaluation. They nevertheless suggest both a substantial initial sampling/deployment gap and additional deterioration under sampled execution. A decoding mismatch alone is therefore unlikely to explain the entire regression. Historical telemetry also predates the exact-terminal-outcome diagnostic fix.

**Early credit is not established by aggregate critic accuracy.** [Actor GAE](../../src/kaggriculture/ppo.py#L65) uses lambda 0.97218 and gamma 1. The direct terminal-reward contribution halves every 24.57 steps and falls to about 0.000000533 after 512 steps. This does not prove opening actions receive no gradient: intermediate value differences can carry the credit. It does mean the opening depends heavily on the accuracy of those differences.

The common-policy critic experiment (`artifacts/probes/entity-review-20260916-remat/common-policy-fit.json`) trains on the same frozen-policy wave and holds out entire physical games. At epoch twenty:

| --- | ---: | ---: |
| All states | 0.1165 | 0.2144 |
| More than 512 steps remaining | 0.0053 | 0.0190 |

Source-read improves overall fitting but barely predicts opening outcomes in this experiment. Its late slice is worse. These results include one-step NextLat and only 65 held-out trajectories, so they do not isolate value-regression capacity or prove a universal architecture ranking. A full-budget actor-lambda-1 comparison is a useful diagnostic: it removes dependence on intermediate value differences at the cost of higher variance. Report opening advantage quality and actual gameplay, not merely lambda-return explained variance. Return-preserving reward redistribution is another candidate if early credit remains weak; [RUDDER](https://arxiv.org/abs/1806.07857) develops this approach for delayed rewards, but implementing it correctly here requires additional learning and validation.

**The strongest actor refactor addresses selected-action conditioning.** [EntityActor](../../src/kaggriculture/entity.py#L625) emits all unit and market scores from the current observation. The [sampler](../../src/kaggriculture/policy.py#L440) then executes decisions in order, updating shared seeds, tile state, inventory and market legality. Later decisions react through their masks; their relative preferences between still-legal alternatives do not change with the selected prefix. Quantity logits condition on the chosen market kind, but not the full sequence of earlier selected actions and resulting ledger. The native sampler likewise consumes precomputed scores before updating masks ([Rust implementation](../../rust/kagg_env/src/core.rs#L1859)). This is a representational restriction, not a demonstrated coding bug. BC already scores 98.05% on the v27 panel with the existing actor, so that actor clearly can represent strong play. The proposed decoder is a coordination research hypothesis, not an established explanation or cure for PPO forgetting.

Use a spatial/economic encoder once per turn, cache its context, then decode units and market orders in engine execution order. Feed each step the selected prefix and the updated resource ledger, including quantity decisions. Train with teacher-forced prefixes and replay those exact prefixes and legality masks for PPO likelihoods. A small causal decoder with cached K/V and a compiled fixed-maximum-length sampling loop avoids rerunning the full encoder for every action. The implementation must preserve public/private boundaries and prove equality of teacher-forced and sequential log probabilities. [Multi-Agent Transformer](https://arxiv.org/abs/2205.14953) provides relevant autoregressive coordination precedent; its cooperative multi-agent results do not establish success in this game.

Judge this decoder on held-out action consistency, intended versus executed actions, resource conflicts, PPO replay parity, and fixed-panel score per GPU-hour. Low BC NLL alone can hide rare but decisive inconsistent decisions. The main risk is sequential rollout overhead, especially if every decoder step adds a host/device round trip. Maintain the encoded context and compact ledger on device or design a fused sampler boundary before committing to the full rewrite.

**The critic should represent valuation directly.** The current critic retains an actor-like decision trunk, then [pools decisions and source memory through one 96-dimensional query](../../src/kaggriculture/entity.py#L728). Source-read removes the exclusive passage through action slots, which is a reasonable promotion. It does not establish that a single pooled vector is the best representation of two interacting economies.

My preferred replacement has separate farm/economy representations and a small interacting set of valuation queries for productive assets, inventories, cash, workforce, market conditions and remaining time. Preserve own/opponent structure until comparison, and provide differences and commitments explicitly. Add predictions of economically meaningful changes at several horizons, with episode-held-out evaluation. Outcome prediction remains the policy objective; auxiliary future-money or production predictions must not silently replace winning with a different reward. This is a hypothesis to test against the source-read critic on common policy data and then full learning runs.

Opponent conditioning is also worth testing: similar opening states can have different continuations against different policies, and the centralized critic does not observe a complete opponent-policy identity. A learned archive-ID lookup risks memorization. Prefer policy-behavior descriptors available at collection time, with held-out opponent evaluation. Actor temporal memory is a separate hypothesis because opponent inventories are hidden; test whether public history adds predictive information before adopting recurrent rollout storage and sequence training.

**Current NextLat does not demonstrate long-range planning.** Production actor NextLat is off. Critic NextLat predicts one step, with latent and decoded-value terms both active. In the final diagnostic sample, the combined prediction-to-persistence loss ratio is 0.987 for control, 0.972 for source-read, and 1.008 for hardness. These small gains, and the hardness regression, do not prove the objective is harmful; they also do not show it is worth its compute or improves early credit. Compare against persistence and shuffled-action baselines over multiple checkpoints. A matched full-run critic-NextLat-off ablation and gradient-conflict measurements should precede retaining it unchanged in a new critic. Multi-horizon event/economic forecasts are a stronger candidate than merely scaling the existing one-step latent predictor.

**The improved league still optimizes difficulty rather than measured learning progress.** Discovery now tests new policies while stale refresh checks for forgetting; known hard opponents retain most slots. Distinct snapshots can nevertheless implement nearly identical strategies. At production shape the league supplies only 64 of 320 learner trajectories, and one v27 lane usually supplies roughly six of those 320. The selector alone cannot make this external opponent dominate the objective. Before changing allocations, measure outcome-vector diversity, per-opponent learning progress and gradient contribution. Compare a controlled larger external-opponent share or behavioral deduplication while preserving unseen-policy discovery and stale checks. Do not treat hardest-opponent sampling as a proof of an optimal curriculum.

**Compute favors focused structural changes.** Recorded steady iteration medians are 11.60 seconds for control, 11.91 for source-read, 19.73 for writeback, 35.98 for tile-bias and 25.66 for bounded BiXT. Their benchmark artifacts (`artifacts/probes/entity-review-20260916-remat/`) and the BiXT benchmark (`artifacts/probes/entity-review-20260916-bixt-bounded/bixt-benchmark.jsonl`) establish cost and feasibility. Updates dominate the measured budget. BiXT logged 44 waves and 754 actor updates, but no completed gameplay evaluation was present at review time. Its expense is not evidence of greater learning capacity. Compare both matched actor-update checkpoints and equal-wall-time runs before favoring any more expensive architecture.

A Dreamer-style recurrent world model is a legitimate total-stack alternative: learn dynamics, imagine continuations and optimize through a learned value model. [DreamerV3](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-025-08744-2) demonstrates this general approach. Here it would also introduce model bias, hidden-opponent inference and opponent nonstationarity before the existing regression is explained. The exact Rust simulator makes hybrid planning over public state another possible route, but hidden information and the joint action space still require a belief/proposal model. Neither warrants promotion on the present evidence.
