# RL review, 2026-09-20: objective, credit, and architecture

Read-only review of the production self-play PPO loop, with emphasis on architecture and openness to total refactors. Four lenses (data pipeline, PPO mechanics, BC initializer, run evidence) were delegated, and the draft was then red-teamed by a fifth agent that checked every cited number against source, checkpoints and journals; its corrections are folded in. Findings are tagged **D** (demonstrated in code or data) or **H** (hypothesis, with the experiment that resolves it). No source file was changed and nothing was trained.

The [review of 2026-09-18](rl-review-2026-09-18.md), the [run comparison](../experiments/run-comparison-2026-09-18.md) and the [structural ablation plan](../experiments/architecture-ablations-2026-09-18.md) remain the record of what has been run. The structural campaign (shared plan, causal execution decoder, forecast critic) has throughput benchmarks but no learning results (`artifacts/probes/structural-gae-20260918/cross-run-comparison.md`) as of today and none of its jobs is queued.

## 1. Verdict

No PPO recipe has improved deployed play over the shared BC initializer on the v27 panel (the starter panel is saturated at 100% argmax for every arm and cannot show a change either way). What the recipes differ in is how fast they lose it. Fixed 256-map argmax panel against scripted-v27, from the credit-valuation panels (`artifacts/probes/credit-valuation-20260918/`) and the [run comparison](../experiments/run-comparison-2026-09-18.md):

| Recipe | Wave | Actor updates | v27 argmax | v27 sampled | Paired bank change vs v27, argmax |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Shared BC initializer | 0 | 0 | 98.05% | 26.17% | 0 |
| NextLat off, original | 35 | 609 | 94.92% | 12.89% | -1.2k |
| NextLat off, replacement | 94 | 2,320 | 91.02% | 3.52% | -9.6k |
| Economic critic, NextLat on | 36 | 696 | 87.89% | 12.50% | -7.0k |
| Lambda 1, NextLat on, original | 137 | 3,538 | 33.20% | 6.25% | -15.8k |
| Lambda 1, NextLat on, replacement | 72 | 1,653 | 0.00% | 0.00% | -53.7k |
| Control / source-read / hardness, NextLat on | 112-125 | 2,755-3,161 | 0.00-1.17% | 0.00-0.39% | -78.5k (source-read) |

Three things in that table drive the diagnosis. First, the retained recipe still bleeds: 9.6k of paired bank against v27 and 14.9k against starter after 94 waves, with the score holding only because the margin was large. Second, sampled play against v27 falls toward zero in every arm while sampled play against starter improves in all eight evaluated arms, by 4 to 11 points in the five credit-valuation arms and 12 to 14 in the three older ones (`artifacts/probes/hardness-source-default-20260918/historical-sampled.json`): PPO is learning something in-distribution (fewer catastrophic self-play games) and losing something out-of-distribution (v27's market). Third, the two lambda-1 runs were launched with identical configuration and the same seed (both journals open with next_seed 20261004), and at wave 72 they sit at entropy 0.149 versus 0.207 and self-play money 54.1k versus 28.4k; they end at 33% and 0%. The loop is nondeterministic under a fixed seed, and its outcome is dominated by that nondeterminism.

The mechanism, as far as the evidence supports it:

1. The reward is the sign of the final bank margin at step 719, exactly zero-sum, and nothing rewards the learner's own economy. In mirror self-play (80% of the data) the learner's economy is in fact flat (self-play money 55.6k in warmup, 50.6k mid-run, 54.3k at the end of the retained run), so the degradation is not a joint decline that the objective cannot see. It is opponent-specific: the learner's play in v27-shaped markets, which the training distribution barely covers, is what erodes.
2. In the first 200 steps (513+ steps to go) the critic explains 0.5 to 1.9% of held-out return variance and 15% online; at 129 to 512 steps to go, about 60%. The terminal reward's direct weight at 400 steps to go is 1.3e-5. Opening credit is the TD residual of a barely-fit function, broadcast unnormalized to 10 to 11 components per state.
3. NorMuon steps have a fixed magnitude by construction, there is no KL anchor, no entropy control, no weight decay, no cooldown in production, and `target_kl` never binds. Realized policy movement is real and measurable (1.6e-3 per wave, about 500x the 5e-7 to 2.8e-6 first-minibatch floor of the same estimator in the current runs), but nothing in the loop distinguishes movement carrying signal from movement carrying noise, and nothing pulls the policy back.
4. The BC actor's sampled losses are a catastrophic tail (59% of losses to v27 end under a quarter of v27's bank) caused by a few departures per game from an open-loop teacher. Training at temperature 1 spends its signal on that tail; deployment is argmax and never sees it.
5. The five v27 games per wave are 95% lost (24 wins in 445 games after wave 21). Under a terminal sign reward every action in a lost trajectory receives the same negative advantage until the critic learns to expect the loss, after which they carry only residual noise. The lane exists to teach v27 play and may instead be unlearning it.

On architecture: the queued campaign targets actor capacity (shared plan, causal decoder) and critic representation (forecast). Capacity is not what is binding: the same actor reproduces the step-indexed teacher trace at 98% argmax. The honest reading of the sampled collapse is conditional incoherence in the factored action head (section 3, D7), which is an architectural defect, but one that the set-valued market head in 5.4 fixes far more cheaply than a sequential decoder. The critic's horizon is the other real architectural gap, and it is an objective problem before it is a representation problem: no critic can explain the opening from a binary outcome 600 steps away. The redesign in section 5 therefore leads with the objective and the advantage estimator and keeps the entity actor.

## 2. The loop, briefly

Two independent 10x10 farms coupled only through a shared market with inventory-dependent price curves ([market rules](../mechanics/market.md)); seeds, animals and land have fixed prices, only products trade, and town demand drains inventory. 720 steps, 30 days of 24 turns; objective is the final bank. Production ([production.py](../../src/kaggriculture/production.py#L25)) collects 128 mirror self-play games (256 learner trajectories) and 64 league games across 11 lanes at temperature 1.0 and deploys with [argmax](../../src/kaggriculture/inference.py#L426). Actor: D96 entity-attention. Critic: independent centralized source-read model with an HL-Gauss head on [-2.2, 2.2]. Reward mode `terminal-outcome`; actor GAE lambda 0.972, critic lambda 1, DAPO clip 0.80/1.28, one epoch, 29 minibatches of 8192, no advantage normalization. NorMuon 1.5e-4 on matrices, Adam 5.25e-5 on embeddings and heads. The retained run's journal shows 2.9 s rollout and 10.7 s update per wave on an RTX 5090; the Rust simulator steps 16,582 PASS-agent games per second.

## 3. Demonstrated findings

**D1. The objective carries no absolute-economy signal.** [`_native_pair_rewards`](../../src/kaggriculture/rollout.py#L1701) emits the terminal utility, takes its sign for `terminal-outcome`, and negates it for the other seat; the `shaped` mode is a bounded margin ratio negated the same way. All three modes are exactly zero-sum. The learner's bank against v27 falls in every arm (table in section 1) while self-play money in the retained run stays within 55.6k to 50.6k as 14-to-30-wave window means (single waves reach 45.4k). In the collapsed source-read arm the learner's v27-panel bank falls 78.5k while v27's rises 48.5k: most of what the learner loses is not captured by the opponent, it is destroyed.

**D2. Opening-phase advantages are residuals of a critic that barely predicts the opening.** With lambda 0.972 the terminal reward's direct weight is exp(400 · ln 0.972183588) = 1.26e-5 at 400 steps to go. The common-policy held-out fit (`artifacts/probes/entity-review-20260916-remat/common-policy-fit.json`) explains 0.53% (entity) and 1.9% (source-read) of return variance at 513+ steps to go. In `no-nextlat-retry1` at wave 110 the pre-update terminal-residual explained variance by time-to-go is 0.53, 0.65, 0.61 and 0.15 for buckets 1-32, 33-128, 129-512 and 513+. Journal advantage std is 0.19 with mean within ±0.007, against a reward of magnitude 1. [Advantages are not normalized](../../src/kaggriculture/ppo.py#L343) and are broadcast to every active component of the state.

**D3. Policy movement is unconditioned on signal and unrestored.** [NorMuon](../../src/kaggriculture/optim.py#L1) keeps singular directions and discards singular values; its second-moment equalization "cannot change the step's size". Adam runs with eps 1e-20; gradients are [intentionally unclipped](../../src/kaggriculture/ppo.py#L344); no KL-to-BC term, no entropy coefficient, weight decay off, cooldown scale 1.0 throughout; `target_kl` 0.03 is "a safety valve the update never reaches" ([train_ppo.py](../../scripts/train_ppo.py#L1279)). In `no-nextlat-retry1` realized approx KL has median 1.6e-3 and max 2.1e-3 per wave; the same estimator on the first minibatch before any step reads 5e-7 to 2.8e-6 after release, and the per-head replay-parity audit 1e-10 to 1e-8, so the movement is real and a KL budget could target it. Rollout entropy goes from 0.127 to 0.162 (peak 0.173) with fluctuations, 0.295 in the collapsed economic arm. A run named `production-anchored-p100-20260911` exists but its arguments contain no KL or anchor term (it is an older structured-architecture run at LR 5e-5), so a KL anchor to the initializer is untried.

**D4. The BC sampled gap is a catastrophic tail, and the teacher is open-loop.** Holdout metrics embedded in the shared BC checkpoint (`artifacts/probes/credit-valuation-20260918/bc-actor.pt`):

| Head | NLL | Top-1 | Entropy (nats) | Components per game |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| unit | 0.00074 | 0.99990 | 0.0020 | 6,649 |
| market kind | 0.00377 | 0.99877 | 0.0090 | 1,311 |
| quantity | 0.00370 | 0.99969 | 0.0157 | 331 |

That implies roughly 2.4 (from top-1) to 3.7 (from entropy) sampled departures from argmax per game on teacher states. Of sampled-BC losses to v27, 59.3% end with under a quarter of v27's bank (46 games under 2k) and 16.4% are near misses; wins have the same 91k median bank as argmax. v27 replays a baked per-step action table; only weed repair, SELL-slot reordering and hand padding read the game ([v27_script.rs](../../rust/kagg_env/src/v27_script.rs)), and weed repair carries hidden memory the observation does not expose ([V27State](../../rust/kagg_env/src/core.rs#L330)). An off-trace unit has no teacher-defined recovery. The clone objective is plain pooled masked NLL ([train_bc.py](../../scripts/train_bc.py#L837)) with no smoothing, entropy term or per-head weighting, trained for two epochs.

**D5. Training and deployment disagree, and the disagreement is where the signal goes.** All eight evaluated arms' sampled scores against starter rise (4 to 14 points; credit-valuation panels and `artifacts/probes/hardness-source-default-20260918/historical-sampled.json`) while their argmax bank against v27 falls. The gradient is buying insurance against departures the deployed policy never makes.

**D6. The league cannot retire a 0% opponent and gives it five games per wave.** PFSP weight is (1 − score)^2 ([league.py](../../src/kaggriculture/league.py#L424)); the production hardness selector ranks by lowest posterior mean ([league.py](../../src/kaggriculture/league.py#L567)), with the same effect. After wave 21 in the retained run v27 receives 5 games per wave and the score on those five is mostly 0 or 0.2 (one win) through wave 98. The critic has no opponent-identity input ([entity.py](../../src/kaggriculture/entity.py#L653)). public-v16 is not in the league and has no Rust port (only v27 does).

**D7. The action interface carries redundancy the likelihood treats as distinct choices.** 36 of 68 unit actions are PICKUP variants that are simultaneously legal whenever the shed holds enough ([actions.py](../../src/kaggriculture/actions.py#L282)). Market slots are a STOP-terminated sequence whose per-slot kind logits come from one forward pass and see earlier slots only through the ledger mask, not the sampled kind ([policy.py](../../src/kaggriculture/policy.py#L579)); a sampled deviation at slot k leaves slot k+1 still pushing the teacher's slot-(k+1) kind. The v16 corpus emits 0.307 duplicate kinds per turn (27,008 of 28,288 duplicates are HIRE), so the same order multiset has many sequence encodings and BC fixed one of them. Quantity uses 100 bins ([constants.py](../../src/kaggriculture/constants.py#L175)) of which the corpus uses 29 values, all at most 46. These are structural facts; their share of entropy growth is H4.

**D8. Capacity is not binding; coherence and critic horizon are the real architectural gaps.** The entity actor reproduces the teacher at 98.05% argmax after two BC epochs. Among evaluated arms the ordering is set by the training recipe (NextLat off versus on, lambda) and by seed, not by actor architecture; BiXT has no completed gameplay evaluation (`artifacts/probes/entity-review-20260916-bixt-bounded/outcome-summary.json`). Source-read did lift held-out critic R² at 129 to 512 steps to go from 0.16 to 0.31, which is the one architecture result with a clear direction.

## 4. Hypotheses and the experiments that resolve them

**H1. Most of the movement is noise-driven drift.** Resolving experiment (hours, existing code path): a production run with advantages randomly permuted across states each wave, destroying signal but preserving its distribution. Its entropy will rise mechanically, so the readout is the v27 argmax panel score over 100 waves: decay on the production schedule means drift, a flat curve means the real signal is what harms. A zeroed-advantage arm is uninformative because it produces no actor step.

**H2. The v27 lane unlearns v27 play.** Resolving experiment (hours): the retained recipe with v27 removed from the league (or its advantages masked to zero), replicated, since a fixed seed does not make two runs comparable here. If the v27 argmax score decays more slowly than with the lane present, all-lost lanes are net negative under the sign reward and the fix is either the objective (5.1) or the lane weighting (5.6).

**H3. Opponent-specific market behaviour is what erodes.** Resolving measurement (hours): on the v27 panel, compare BC and the retained checkpoint on sell timing, realized prices per product, hire counts and land purchases, paired by map. Erosion concentrated in market timing under v27's price pressure, with farm operations unchanged, confirms it and says the critic's market representation, not the farm representation, is where capacity should go.

**H4. An asset-inclusive own-economy potential produces informative advantages where the margin sign does not.** Job 6097 (docs/experiments/runs.md) showed the liquid-only shaped potential penalizing buy_land, buy_animal and buy_seed. It ran at gamma 0.997 with a log-relative potential whose per-dollar penalty was 1/(3000 + money), and reward shaping was rejected as the cause of that era's regression on 2026-09-10 (docs/experiments/runs.md, "Optimizer alignment"). The proposal in 5.1 is a different objective, not a reshaping of the margin, and the evidence for it is indirect (D1, D2, D5); it is a judgment call. Resolving experiment (a day): the reward of 5.1 with the existing loop; readouts are training money, v27 argmax score and the sign of the advantage on investment actions.

**H5. Drift control alone preserves the initializer's strength but does not improve it.** Resolving experiment (a day, same seed): matrix LR divided by four with cooldown, KL-to-BC anchor on replayed rows, and entropy penalty toward the BC entropy. Success is a flat v27 argmax bank and score over 120 waves.

**H6. Entropy growth concentrates in the market kind head.** The journal records only pooled entropy. Resolving measurement (hours): replay checkpoints on a fixed state panel and report per-head entropy and the fraction of turns whose order multiset matches the BC actor's while the sequence does not.

**H7. A sharper clone of the production actor closes most of the sampled gap.** The 11-epoch clone in `runs/rl-repair-schema2-bc` is a different architecture (structured, schema 2, D80, 8 layers) and cannot be evaluated on the current panel; its 18.6x unit, 3.5x kind and 88x quantity entropy reductions are only suggestive. Resolving experiment (hours of GPU): retrain the entity clone for 8 to 12 epochs and evaluate sampled and argmax on the 256-map panel.

**H8. Counterfactual branched advantages give per-component credit with far lower variance than GAE.** Resolving experiment (a week, 5.2): prototype the branch API and measure the correlation between branched advantages and the actual outcome change when the branched action is imposed, against the same correlation for GAE advantages, on frozen-policy waves.

## 5. The redesign

Ordered by expected return per unit of work. Each item stands alone; together they form the stack in 5.7.

### 5.1 Objective: own economy first, opponent second

Replace the single zero-sum sign with a mixed objective:

    reward_t = Phi_own(t+1) − Phi_own(t) − beta · (Phi_opp(t+1) − Phi_opp(t))
    terminal = outcome sign (kept), scaled

Phi is money plus the value of seeds, animals, land, planted crops and shed products, with the non-liquid part multiplied by a schedule that reaches zero at the last step so Phi_T equals the bank. With gamma 1 the potential terms telescope to Phi_T − Phi_0, so the full-horizon optimum of the own-economy part is exactly "maximize final bank"; beta sets how much the learner is asked to hurt the opponent through the market. Start beta at 0.25.

Design details that decide whether this works, learned from job 6097:

- Value shed products at what the market would pay for the whole stack under the current curve (exact and cheap in Rust), not at a fixed price, so gluts are visible.
- Value seeds, animals and land at cost, so buying is potential-neutral and only growth, harvest, death and waste move Phi. Cost-basis valuation makes unused seeds invisible until the schedule bleeds them; that is a mild and correct late penalty.
- HIRE has no asset counterpart. A hand's wage is a pure expense; its value shows up only through the crops it tends. Under lambda 0.972 that payback (48 to 240 steps) is mostly outside the credit window, so this proposal should be paired with a longer actor lambda or with 5.2, not run at 0.972 alone.
- Keep Phi linear in money. The 6097 potential was log-relative and its asymmetry was the defect.
- The telescoping guarantee is for the full return; under lambda 0.972 the local shape of Phi is what the actor sees, which is why the shape must be economically sensible rather than merely invariant.

Cost: a new reward mode with per-seat potentials (today `_native_pair_rewards` hardcodes antisymmetry via `column_stack(r, −r)` and Rust exposes one pair potential), a re-ranged HL-Gauss support, and an exact null test: a fixed policy's summed reward must equal its final bank minus its initial potential. Tradeoffs: a learner optimizing its own bank may under-invest in market interference; D1 says interference is second-order in this game and beta plus the terminal term cover it.

### 5.2 Credit: simulator-branched counterfactual advantages under the deployed policy

The simulator is exact, deterministic given its seed, clonable ([`Game` derives Clone](../../rust/kagg_env/src/core.rs#L250)) and fast. Stop inferring Q from 719-step returns and measure it:

1. Roll the base trajectory with the learner at argmax (the deployed policy) against a league or snapshot opponent.
2. At B branch points per trajectory (32, importance-sampled toward high-entropy states), clone the game and impose one deviation on one component, drawn from a tempered proposal q over the legal set.
3. Continue the branch at argmax for H steps (24 to 48), then bootstrap with the critic's own-economy value from 5.1.
4. Advantage = branch H-step return + bootstrap − the base trajectory's identical quantity at the same step. The base is the argmax continuation, so this is Q(s, a') − Q(s, a*) with the opponent held fixed.
5. Update log pi(a'|s) with that advantage. This is the ordinary policy gradient with Q(s, a*) as baseline: a draw of a' = a* contributes zero, so no branch is needed for it, and the argmax action's probability moves only through normalization.

This is the "vine" estimator of TRPO ([Schulman et al. 2015](https://arxiv.org/abs/1502.05477)) with the greedy policy as the base, and COMA's counterfactual baseline ([Foerster et al. 2018](https://arxiv.org/abs/1705.08926)) computed exactly instead of learned. It answers the question that matters for an argmax deployment: would this deviation from my greedy plan have helped, on this map, against this opponent? Per-component credit is native; the broadcast in D2 disappears; the sampled-tail problem in D5 disappears because the base is greedy and exploration lives in branches.

What it does not do, and the honest accounting:

- **It does not remove bootstrap bias.** A 24 to 48 step branch delegates the same long paybacks to the critic that lambda 0.972 does. It removes sampling variance and gives per-component credit, which is what D2's noise floor needs, but investment credit still depends on the critic's own-economy value being good at 1 to 2 days out. That is a much easier target than a binary outcome 600 steps out, and it is why 5.1 is a prerequisite. For calibration, run a small fraction of branches to the end of the game (4 per trajectory, both seats forwarded, averaging about 360 steps to go, is roughly 3x the base cost) and check the truncated estimates against them.
- **Common random numbers are partial.** The daily RNG is reseeded from (seed, day) ([core.rs](../../rust/kagg_env/src/core.rs#L2557)), so the stream is shared between base and branch, but weed draws are consumed only for empty tiles and the shop draw follows, so a deviation that changes any tile's emptiness shifts later draws that day. Exact CRN would need per-tile draws, which diverges from the official engine's consumption order pinned by the parity tests. Accept the partial version.
- **The importance weight pi(a')/q(a') is about 1e-4 × |legal| for exactly the deviations the current policy rejects.** Either those get no signal or the weight is clipped, and a clipped weight makes this expert-iteration-style policy improvement rather than an unbiased on-policy gradient. Name it as such and accept it: the policy is being asked to adopt deviations that measurably beat its greedy plan.
- **Argmax mirror self-play is degenerate.** Both farms are built identically, so a deterministic policy plays the same game from both seats. Draw the opponent seat from the snapshot archive and league lanes, and keep a fraction of base games at low temperature for critic coverage.
- **Branch continuations condition on the opponent's true hidden state**, so single-branch advantages are hindsight-conditioned. This is the same asymmetry the centralized critic already carries and averages out over maps and seats.

Cost per wave with B = 32 and H = 24, once the opponent seat comes from the archive rather than the mirror: 320 learner trajectories need 320 games, so base forwards are about 460k (learner plus neural opponent) against today's 230k, and branches add 320 × 32 × 24 × 2 ≈ 491k, plus 768 sequential batched environment steps at launch-bound cost. The rollout phase grows three to four times from 2.9 s, to roughly 10 s. The actor update touches 10k branch-point states instead of 230k. The critic trains on the 230k base states plus only the 10k branch-point states with their H-step targets, not the branch continuations, so its cost is unchanged; if actor and critic minibatch costs are roughly equal (the only profile is a stale one of the structured actor, `artifacts/probes/update-profile-20260910.json`, 80 ms and 74 ms forward-backward), the 10.7 s update roughly halves. Net iteration time is about the same as today, with the balance shifted from update to rollout. Ragged branch sets need padding to fixed shapes to avoid recompiles under reduce-overhead mode, and the replay-parity audit needs a q-likelihood path.

Implementation: a `BatchEnv.branch` entry in [python.rs](../../rust/kagg_env/src/python.rs) cloning games at given indices into a branch pool, the existing external-action step ([python.rs L1007](../../rust/kagg_env/src/python.rs#L1007)), and a Python driver batching base and branch forwards together. Correctness gates: a null branch reproduces the base bit-for-bit; imposed-action replay reproduces the per-component log-probabilities the update recomputes. Roughly a week with tests.

### 5.3 Drift control, immediately

Independent of everything else, add a restoring force so that noise cannot walk the policy away from a good solution:

- KL(pi_ref || pi) on the replayed rows, coefficient set so the realized KL to the reference stays below a few e-3 across 100 waves; reference is BC until PPO produces a better panel checkpoint, then that.
- Matrix LR 4e-5 with cooldown enabled, as the cheap alternative.
- Signal-to-noise gating on NorMuon: scale the orthogonalized step by the cosine agreement between two half-minibatch gradients, clipped to [0, 1], so a step with no signal is a small step. This restores the property Adam has and Muon deliberately removed.

Expected benefit: a flat v27 argmax bank and score instead of the current slow bleed, which is also the precondition for measuring whether any later change improves play. Tradeoff: anchoring to an open-loop teacher freezes its mistakes; loosen the anchor once 5.1 and 5.2 give the policy something better to move toward.

### 5.4 Action interface: remove the redundancy

- Market orders as a set rather than a sequence: one head per quantified kind emitting a count (0 meaning no order) in canonical order, plus a HIRE count and a BUY_LAND flag. The environment processes slots in order, so a fixed canonical order is a mild restriction on money-constrained sequencing that v16's duplicate HIRE orders suggest it does not exploit. This removes the STOP-slot, permutation and duplicate-kind redundancy of D7, turns the per-turn market likelihood into 22 conditionally independent terms, and lets the causal-decoder question be dropped rather than answered.
- Factor PICKUP into a pickup flag with item and count sub-heads, turning 36 of 68 unit actions into one plus two small heads.
- Relative quantities (sell all, sell half, keep n) alongside absolute bins; the corpus uses 29 of 100 bins.

Cost: BC dataset re-extraction with a canonicalization pass, new heads, mask code in Python and Rust, replay-parity tests. Benefit: fewer ways for entropy to grow without behaviour changing, better-conditioned per-component credit, and the conditional-coherence defect behind the sampled collapse addressed without a sequential decoder. This is the architecture change the evidence supports.

### 5.5 Critic: an economic valuation head

Keep the source-read trunk. Give it two heads, own-economy return (5.1) and margin, both HL-Gauss, trained on base states and branch-point states under 5.2. Add the multi-horizon money, production and price forecasts the structural campaign already implemented as auxiliaries; with a dense economic reward they are aligned with the value target rather than a substitute for it. Provide an opponent behaviour descriptor from the opponent's public actions so far (order-mix histogram, hire rate, land count) so league lanes are distinguishable without an archive-ID lookup. If H3 confirms that market behaviour is what erodes, spend critic capacity on the market tokens, not the farm.

### 5.6 League and data

Weight lanes by expected advantage information, p(1 − p) under the current posterior, with a floor for discovery and stale refresh, instead of lowest posterior mean; an opponent the learner loses to 100% carries no contrast under a sign reward, and H2 tests whether it carries harm. Port public-v16 to Rust the way v27 was (an open-loop table with its front-running state) and add it as a built-in lane; that is a porting job, not a config change. Under 5.1 and 5.2 lost league games become informative, which shrinks this lever, but the current rule is actively wasteful.

### 5.7 The stack as a whole

Objective: own final bank with a small margin term, dense through an asset-inclusive potential. Credit: branched counterfactual advantages under the greedy policy, critic-bootstrapped at one to two days, per component, with end-of-game branches for calibration. Policy: the existing entity actor with a set-valued market head and factored pickup. Critic: source-read trunk, own-economy and margin heads, forecast auxiliaries, opponent descriptor. Optimization: SNR-gated NorMuon or a KL anchor, cooldown on. Data: greedy base play against archive and league opponents with exploration confined to branches, plus a low-temperature fraction for coverage. League: information-weighted lanes including a ported v16. Evaluation: the fixed 256-map argmax panel against v27, v16 and starter, reporting paired bank as well as score, since score alone hid a 9.6k bleed for 94 waves. Every arm needs replicates: the 33% versus 0% pair ran under one seed, so the loop is nondeterministic and a single run cannot rank recipes at the effect sizes in play.

Deliberately excluded: a recurrent actor (excluded by design; public history is in the observation), a learned world model (the exact simulator makes it unnecessary and branching gets the benefit without model bias), and the causal execution decoder (5.4 removes the redundancy that motivated it at a fraction of the cost). The shared-plan actor is not ruled out, but it is a capacity change and capacity is not what is binding.

| 7 | Branch API and counterfactual advantages, greedy base | H8, D2, D5 | 1 week | Branched advantage predicts imposed-action outcome change; argmax score and bank rise above BC |
| 8 | Set-valued market head, factored pickup, BC re-extraction | D7, H6 | 1 week | Lower BC NLL at the same argmax score; entropy flat under PPO |
| 9 | Economic critic heads, opponent descriptor, information-weighted league, v16 port | D6 | 3 to 5 days | Held-out own-economy R² above 0.5 at 513+ steps to go; league lanes carry non-zero advantage |

Items 1 to 4 need no new training code. Items 5 and 6 use existing reward and optimizer entry points plus a new reward mode. Item 7 is the large one and is where the deployed policy first receives a gradient about the deployed policy. Item 8 is the architecture work the evidence justifies. Run every arm, including the single-run decay comparisons in items 1, 2 and 6, as replicates; the fixed seed does not make runs reproducible. Do not spend GPU time on the queued structural arms until 5 and 6 have produced a run whose v27 argmax bank does not bleed; an architecture comparison on a decaying baseline measures forgetting rate, not learning.

## 7. Throughput

Learning, not throughput, is binding. The rollout phase is under a third of the wave and the update phase over two thirds; under 5.2 the actor update shrinks by an order of magnitude, the critic update stays (it gains only branch-point states), and the rollout grows three to four times, so the redesign is roughly throughput-neutral at production shape. No hardware or batching change is recommended.
