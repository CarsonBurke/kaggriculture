**What survives the confounds.** BC has a large sampling weakness before PPO starts. PPO then loses additional v27 strength: the recovered replacement checkpoints' paired sampled-score differences from BC are −26.17, −22.66 and −13.67 percentage points respectively; all exploratory 95% game-seed bootstrap intervals exclude zero. These intervals concern these checkpoint snapshots, not training-seed uncertainty or causal effects of architecture. Historical control/source-read/hardness checkpoints also score 0%, 0% and 0.39% under matched sampled evaluation (`artifacts/probes/hardness-source-default-20260918/historical-sampled.json`). The regression is therefore not solely an argmax deployment mismatch.

NextLat-off preserves greedy play comparatively well, but its sampled policy still deteriorates severely. Removing NextLat also changes minibatch organization, so this is not a pure auxiliary-gradient ablation. Economic critic training remains too short and unmatched to establish its learning advantage. Lambda 1 is not a sufficient remedy in the observed runs. Starter sampled scores improve while v27 scores worsen; an aggregate score or training money can conceal that tradeoff. None of this proves the model cannot eventually improve with further training, nor supports promoting a new architecture now.
