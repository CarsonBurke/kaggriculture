Use **hardness + source-read + NextLat off** as the common baseline selected by the [run comparison](RUN_COMPARISON_20260918.md). The next campaign should change how decisions are coordinated and how future consequences are represented. The three designs below have code implementations, with queued compiled correctness tests. No new learning result is claimed until the compiled correctness gates and full training comparisons finish.

The task has three structural properties. Many decisions share seeds, inventory, labor and cash within a turn. Production commitments have delayed returns across a 720-step game. Opponent behavior and hidden inventory change the value of apparently similar public states. The current actor computes preferences before the selected action prefix exists; its masks enforce feasibility but cannot revise relative preferences among still-legal actions. Its critic predicts one value from a snapshot and assigns the same state advantage to many distinct choices. Those interfaces motivate the following ablations a priori. The run evidence determines the starting recipe and priority, not whether these hypotheses are already true.

**A — Global strategic workspace with a sampled shared plan. Implement first.**

Factor the actor into a public spatial/economic encoder, a small interacting global workspace, a categorical turn plan, and local unit/market decoders. Start with eight learned workspace states and eight plan choices. The expensive encoder runs once; a sampled plan conditions every unit, order-kind and quantity head. Keep exact native sequential masks and resource accounting. This replaces decision-slot-only reasoning with an explicit shared strategic representation and correlates stochastic choices across the turn.

The specific hypothesis is that independent noisy preferences combine into incoherent plans even when the greedy policy is strong. The BC gap of 98% argmax versus 26% sampled makes this worth testing. A shared plan can choose a consistent buying/production/worker-allocation pattern while retaining cheap parallel head computation. Learned plan labels need not correspond to hand-written strategies.

Train BC by exactly marginalizing the eight plan probabilities over the demonstrated legal action sequence, using stable log-sum-exp and sharing encoded observations across branches. This avoids inventing plan labels. PPO records the selected plan, its old probability, and every conditional action probability. Recompute the augmented joint probability during replay; never score a sampled plan as if the policy emitted only the marginal action distribution. Apply the same joint PPO surrogate to the baseline used for this comparison. Joint clipping may be harder in a large action space; that is a separately measured control, not a silent optimizer change.

The required controls are the current actor with joint PPO, and the same workspace/decoder with one deterministic plan. This separates a changed surrogate, global reasoning, and correlated exploration. Architectures receive equal BC data and budgets; report initialization quality separately from RL gains. If the plan collapses to equivalent branches, record that result rather than imposing artificial utilization objectives to hide it. One plan per turn does not create long-term memory.

Keep plan sampling and decoder conditioning on GPU with one existing native transfer per turn. Vectorize the small branch dimension for BC. Report sampled and greedy score, score change from initialization, plan utilization, resource conflicts, long coherent production sequences, update throughput and total GPU time. The architecture wins only if coherent stochastic behavior produces better gameplay at useful compute cost.

**B — Fully conditional execution-order actor. Compare against A as an alternative.**

Replace all fixed precomputed scores with a causal decoder over sixteen unit choices and ten market kind/quantity pairs: 36 factors. Each factor reads the actual selected prefix, an exact predecision resource ledger and cached encoder context. Later preferences can then respond to actual earlier choices. This is more expressive than a shared plan and has a distinct systems cost.

The [detailed decoder design](ARCHITECTURE_DECODER_DESIGN.md) specifies native ledger parity, integer pricing, inventory insertion order, STOP/padding, actor privacy, BC teacher forcing, replay storage and export. Use parallel teacher-forced neural evaluation during updates and cached K/V generation during rollouts. Ledger preprocessing still follows execution order. Generation must stay on device throughout the turn: 36 Python/native round trips per step would make the design uncompetitive.

Test B against the same joint-PPO baseline as A. Preserve the outcome objective and source-read critic with auxiliaries off. Compare prefix-conditioned likelihoods and sampled gameplay, not only BC marginal NLL. A changed future suffix must never affect an earlier conditional probability; sequential and teacher-forced probabilities must agree under compiled BF16. The device ledger must agree with native masks across full games and corner cases before learning.

The decision is whether direct selected-prefix conditioning earns its sequential generation cost over the global-plan alternative. Do not combine A and B initially: they answer related questions, and combining them first would obscure which mechanism helps.

**C — Feed-forward dual-economy critic with multi-horizon forecasting.**

Use an independently parameterized centralized economic trunk with 24 typed states: both farms, both workforces, nine products, three animal types, five crop types, two farm summaries and the town. The value head reads these state-only representations. A separate forecasting branch reads the current selected actions and predicts observable economic changes at 1, 24, 96 and 384 steps. This is entirely feed-forward: no recurrent states, sequence burn-in or history caches.

Train on observed future-state deltas with fixed feature scales and validity masks that exclude terminal crossings, missing states and unavailable horizons. The representation is shared with value fitting, but the value head never sees the current actions. Keep the existing GAE estimators, lambda values and terminal-outcome objective unchanged. Forecast loss is an auxiliary signal, not a replacement reward or a prefix-dependent baseline.

Compare the ordinary source-read critic, the same economic trunk without forecasting heads, and the forecasting critic with coefficient one. All reuse identical baseline BC actor bytes. This separates typed economic reasoning from the prediction task. Measure held-out forecast error against persistence, action-shuffling sensitivity, early outcome calibration and gameplay per GPU-hour. Reduced auxiliary error alone does not earn promotion. Ordinary shuffled PPO minibatches remain valid because there is no temporal hidden state to reconstruct.

**Rejected arms.** The opponent-conditioned prefix critic is removed: its proposed estimator changed GAE/credit assignment, which is outside this campaign. Recurrence is excluded throughout, including the forecasting model. The causal actor's within-turn action prefix is discarded after every action and carries no memory across environment turns.

**Execution and selection.** Implement A first because it probes coordinated exploration at modest rollout overhead. B is the direct, more expressive actor alternative. C independently tests economic representations and multi-horizon supervision with the existing GAE estimator. Each structural arm gets its explicitly named controls. Run families sequentially rather than launch a Cartesian product. Combine mechanisms only after their isolated comparison earns it.

Use one training seed and the full production workload: 128 self-play plus 64 league games, B8192, 720 episode steps, compiled BF16. Critic-only arms reuse identical BC bytes; actor changes use the same corpus and BC budget with their own initial evaluation. Freeze source/configuration/checkpoint identities. Compare at common actor-update milestones and common GPU-time budgets, showing the complete trajectories as well as checkpoint scores. Use the existing 500-wave full learning budget with a predeclared time ceiling; the ceiling is a resource limit, not a matched-update claim. Do not infer failure from a deliberately short training pilot.

Keep both sampled and argmax fixed panels and opponent-specific scores. After fixed-panel selection, run the leading trained candidates head-to-head, since beating the same scripted opponent does not establish their ordering against each other. Use fresh held-out maps for the final choice. Critic diagnostics, money and auxiliary losses explain outcomes but do not decide the winner alone. Record results even when training terminates early: evaluate after terminal state with a valid saved checkpoint, including culls, and avoid making entity controls depend on unrelated BiXT memory gates.

Retain numerical safety and useful culling, but replace the old standalone thirty-wave money/value-loss plateau rule for this campaign. It would have rejected runs that later improved. Cull on sustained fixed-panel deterioration plus stalled relevant learning diagnostics after the declared comparison milestones; preserve the best evaluated checkpoint and report the rule in the manifest before submission. Benchmark full workloads for feasibility and throughput before training. All GPU work goes through MLQ.

Further generic widening, more BiXT latents, plain memory writeback and extra one-step latent prediction are lower priority. Existing results give them weaker practical support than architectures that explicitly represent shared plans, action prefixes and future economic consequences.

**Implementation and execution status.** The registered actor families are `strategic-plan` (eight workspace states, configurable plan count) and `causal-execution` (two causal decoder layers, with the ordinary four-layer critic retained). The forecasting arm is `entity-attention --critic-architecture forecast`; its representation control is `--critic-architecture economic`. Defaults remain the evidence-selected entity actor, source-read critic, hardness league and NextLat off.

The causal path exports a versioned 593-integer initial policy ledger, samples all 36 factors on device, and passes the selected actions directly into buffered native stepping. PPO and BC teacher-force recorded actions with parallel causal attention; neither stores neural hidden states across turns. Native-generated integer price tables are shared across replicas and packaged for inference. Shared-plan BC marginalizes the full demonstrated turn over plans, while PPO records the sampled plan as an explicit joint factor.

`queue_structural_campaign.py` freezes source and constructs separate actor, critic and causal experiment families. Every training arm has its own full-production memory/likelihood gate. Evaluations depend on terminal job state so valid culled checkpoints remain measurable. The training panel runs every 25 actor-active waves; after 150 waves, it may cull a policy that remains at least five score points below its initialization and has spent 100 waves without a material improvement in either smoothed panel score or sampled-policy critic MSE. The thresholds are 0.01 score and 0.01 MSE. R² is reported separately because it is undefined on constant-outcome panels.

The compiled GPU contracts are queued through MLQ. Model-free and native rule tests establish contracts, not architecture strength. No new architecture is promoted before the matched gameplay comparisons finish.
